Store art and trailer

The store pictures and the trailer are our own work, made from the app itself (tools/store/). The covers and the icon show only our own red cell and platelet models. The screenshots and the trailer are pictures of the app, so they show some of the files above. This table names the files under attribution licences that they show, with the licence and the changes. They also show public-domain micrographs (screenshots 1 and 6, and the trailer's eye shot) and a CC0 PDB structure (screenshot 8, and the trailer's cell shot). The trailer's sound is the app's own title music.

Our own work
